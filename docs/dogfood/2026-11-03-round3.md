## Findings (ranked)

### P0

#### R3-01 · Chat: the README/DEMO conversation fails at turn 2 on a fresh seed
- **Steps:** Run `pnpm seed:demo` and open /chat. Ask "What do we owe and where?", which replays. In the same thread ask "Explain check #10482", then "Draft the due-diligence letter for #10482" (exactly the docs/DEMO.md 1:05 scene). Separately, in a new thread, ask "Close out California for this cycle".
- **Expected:** All three demo turns replay, and the close-out plan card renders.
- **Actual:** Turn 2: "Demo data changed since these answers were recorded…". Turn 3: "Offline mode: there is no recorded answer". CA close-out: "Demo data changed…". Close out TX, the single-turn "Explain check #10482" and "What's due in the next 90 days?" all replay fine. Confirmed on three fresh seeds, including `bf4612d`.
- **Root cause (verified):** `scripts/record-chat-cassettes.ts` records `SINGLE_TURNS` first. That list includes "Draft the due-diligence letter for #10482" and "Generate the CA report", which **mutate the DB**. It then records `DEMO_CONVERSATION` and the close-out prompts on that mutated DB. So the recorded `explain_item` result contains `"outreach":[{"channel":"letter","status":"draft"}]`, the demo draft turn recorded `"created":false`, and the CA close-out was recorded with a CA report already generated. I confirmed this by doing it in that order: after a single-turn "Draft … #10482", the 3-turn demo replays fully, and after "Generate the CA report", "Close out California" replays. `chat:verify` passes only because it runs in the same order.
- **Fix:** Reseed (or snapshot and restore) before each recording group, recording the demo conversation and the close-out prompts on a pristine seed, and make `--verify` do the same. A cheaper patch is to record the demo conversation first.
- **Shots:** `01-chat-demo-turn2-drift.png`, `02-chat-closeout-ca-drift.png`
- **Files:** `scripts/record-chat-cassettes.ts:20-31,81-90`, `fixtures/cassettes/chat/*`, `src/lib/chat/agent.ts:69-82`, `docs/DEMO.md:26`

## What worked well (worth keeping in the demo)

- **Payee merge.** The preview shows exactly which item moves, and the result line reads "1 item moved · owner states unchanged · 6 ledger lines relinked · recorded as a human decision".
- **Overdue (R2-02).** The red band on the dashboard reads as a product. Reports' "123 days late" with the counsel caveat is exactly right.

- **Allowlist gate and owner link.** Both work, and the owner link is single-use.

## Biggest opportunities (for hackathon judges)
1. **Make "Close out California" the hero, and make it bulletproof.** The guided close-out card (2 of 8 done, next step, money at stake, links into Approvals, Rules and Reports) is the most "agentic" screen in the app, and right now it fails on a fresh seed (R3-01). Fix the recording order, then let each step's button *do* the step: compose all, approve, generate, run the filing agent. A judge can then watch a state close out in about 90 s.
2. **Lead with late money.** The TX item is 123 days late and the app already computes TX interest ($26.07 on the dashboard). Put both in the Overdue band ("$773.74 late, ~$X interest accruing") next to a one-click **Generate & file catch-up report** that runs the filing agent. "We found you're late and fixed it before the auditor did" is the best 20 seconds a finance judge could see.
3. **Unblock TX through the human-in-the-loop rule flow.** Add "Use researched text" on a null rule value. It shows the three cited sources, a proposed owner-facing sentence and a diff, then Approve, and then the UI shows "15 TX letters can now be drafted". That one click shows citations, human verification and rules feeding letters in a single flow (R3-05).
4. **One notice per owner, as a batch desk.** After the Bayline merge the payee has two checks ($6,126) but would get two letters. Consolidate open checks per payee into one letter, sequence the channels (letter, then email after 14 days, then call), and wire the existing `compose-all` route to a "Compose all, review, approve 12 ready, send" bar (R2-04, R3-13). That turns Approvals from a list into a work queue.
5. **Agent theater with the noise removed.** The filing agent already records video and 5 screenshots. Show them inline in Reports as a filmstrip that stops on "Paused at Approve submission, waiting for you". Hide guard and HMR noise (R3-08), and in offline mode label the runs "scripted replay" (R3-03, R3-09). Close the owner loop on screen the same way: the owner clicks the link, a "Reissue $2,194.70 to Ravenhill" task appears in Needs you, and Mark resolved finishes it (R3-10).
