# Dogfood round 4: 2026-11-03

**Tester persona:** a hackathon judge from finance/ops walking the demo path, then a controller closing out a cycle.
**Build:** `main` at `5c22f2f` (one notice per owner, merged 11:49 PT). The "Vault Light" UI v3 redesign plus the late-money and catch-up filing slices are all in.
**Setup:** I ran `pnpm seed:demo` into `.data/pglite-dogfood4` with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=` (blank) and `ESCHEAT_OFFLINE=1` (47 items, $61,915.00, classifier `heuristic`). I ran one dev server on :3800 with `APP_PASSCODE` set (so the login flow was exercised) and `NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-dogfood4`. Halfway through I reseeded once so I could test chat and the round-3 re-checks on a pristine seed. The first seed attempt, without blanking the key, is finding R4-11.
**Method:** throwaway Playwright scripts (`@axe-core/playwright`, legacy mode) covering 26 routes at 1440, 1024 and 375 (375 with touch and mobile). For each route I captured console and HTTP errors, horizontal overflow, axe serious/critical issues, disclaimer presence, UNVERIFIED count, off-palette computed colors and running animations. I then ran six flow scripts (landing → login → dashboard → items → payees → approvals → rules adopt → reports + catch-up + filing agent → recoveries → owner link → chat → demo clock +1y → compose-all + bulk approve) and a reduced-motion pixel-diff pass. Screenshots are in `.data/shots/dogfood4/` (100 PNGs, 25 MB). `a-*` files are full-page route captures, `f*-`/`r-`/`v-` files are flow and viewport captures.

**Headline:** this is the cleanest round so far. Across 78 route renders there were **0 console errors, 0 hydration warnings, 0 horizontal overflow, 0 axe serious/critical violations**, and the disclaimer appeared on every page, including the replica portals and the owner pages. Reduced motion is honored (the landing WebGL canvas is dropped, and the Sankey particles freeze). The dev-server log was clean apart from the expected wrong-passcode line. The demo conversation and the CA close-out now replay on a fresh seed. The remaining problems are about **meaning**, not mechanics: the one-click TX unlock puts the wrong words in owner letters, the scripted chat drafts a letter the rest of the app says "may not count", and several counts disagree between the pipeline, the dashboard and the items list.

**Severity scale (same as round 3):** P0 breaks the demo path. P1 is wrong or unsafe behavior a judge could hit. P2 is a visible inconsistency or broken polish. P3 is minor or copy.

#### R4-01 · "Use official text" for TX puts holder-addressed wording into every owner letter
- **Page:** Rules → TX → Due diligence → Required notice language → **Use official text**; then Approvals.
- **Steps:** Fresh seed. Open `/rules?state=TX&field=dueDiligence.requiredLanguage`, click **Use official text**, then **Approve official text** (the approver name is prefilled "Demo reviewer"). Advance the demo clock +1y, go to Approvals, click **Compose all**, then open Emberstone Cabinetry's letter.
- **Expected:** An owner-facing notice that meets the Comptroller requirement, written to the owner ("We are holding property for you…").
- **Actual:** The letter to Emberstone Cabinetry reads: *"(1) You (the holder) are holding the property; and (2) You (the holder) may be required to deliver the property to the Comptroller's office on or before July 1…"*. The adoption dialog does warn about this ("addressed to the holder… will appear word for word in every owner letter… Approve only if that is what you want owners to read"), but adoption is **verbatim and not editable** ("AFTER (VERBATIM, NOT EDITABLE)"), so the only way to unblock TX produces a letter that tells the owner *they* are the holder. The bulk-approve dialog then lists 5 such TX letters for one-click approval. The same flow is the "rules feed letters" moment judges are meant to see.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/shots/dogfood4/f2-04-adopt-dialog.png`, `f7-01-tx-letter.png`, `f6-02-bulk-approve-dialog.png`
- **Suggested fix:** Separate the *requirement* (the cited source text, verbatim, kept on the rule) from the *rendering* (an owner-facing sentence). In the adoption dialog, show the verbatim quote plus a proposed owner-facing paraphrase ("Northwind Fixtures, Inc. is holding this property. We may be required to deliver it to the Texas Comptroller on or before July 1 if it is not claimed."), let the reviewer edit that paraphrase, and store both (`requiredLanguage` + `requiredLanguageSource`). Alternatively, keep verbatim adoption but have `compose.ts` substitute the holder's name for "You (the holder)" and say so in the preview's "How this letter was built" note.
- **Files:** `src/app/(app)/rules/adoption-review.tsx`, `src/lib/rules/adopt.ts`, `src/lib/letters/compose.ts`, `rules/TX.json` (`dueDiligence.requiredLanguage`)
