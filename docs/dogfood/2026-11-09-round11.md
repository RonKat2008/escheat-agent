# Dogfood round 11: 2026-11-09 (the Ask wave)

**Tester persona:** a skeptical demo judge, and a finance lead opening Escheat Agent for the first time with no API key. This round I also acted as the auditor who later compares two close packs.
**Build:** `e92da9f` (main after Ask slices 1–5: the mode switch, keyless setup cards, approval cards, the calm dashboard and the round-10 polish), run as a **dev server** (`next dev`).

**Setup:**
- I mirrored the e2e `ui` group (`scripts/e2e-server.ts`): `PGLITE_DATA_DIR=.data/pglite-dogfood11 NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-dogfood11 DEMO_MODE=1 DEMO_ANCHOR_DATE=2026-11-01 AGENTS_ENABLED=1`. Every provider key was blank (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, Lob, AgentMail, Vapi, QBO, Plaid, `DATABASE_URL`), and `ESCHEAT_OFFLINE` was unset. That is the "no key at all" path.
- I used the e2e test secrets for `OWNER_LINK_SECRET`, `MCP_TOKEN`, `ALLOWED_TEST_RECIPIENTS` and `AGENTMAIL_INBOX_ID`, and a throwaway `APP_PASSCODE`. I signed in through `/login`.
- Seed: `pnpm seed:demo` gave 47 items and $61,915.00 (heuristic classifier, eval 98.5%), followed by `scripts/ui-b-prep.ts 0`. Then `next dev -p 3997`.
- I held the `/private/tmp/escheat-e2e.lock` mkdir lock for as long as the server ran. Afterwards I stopped the server and released the lock. I deleted `.next-dogfood11`, `.data/pglite-dogfood11` and the agent artifacts my filing run wrote, and restored `tsconfig.json`, which `next dev` had rewritten.
- The approver name was "Dogfood Controller". A second name, "Second Person", was used only for the double-approval probe.

**Method:** throwaway Playwright scripts in the session scratch area (not committed), chromium, `@axe-core/playwright` with `setLegacyMode(true)` and WCAG 2.0/2.1 A and AA tags. Widths were 1440 and 375 (375 with mobile and touch emulation).

### P1

#### R11-01 · A closed month is restated by a later backdated import, and its "closed" card shows the new numbers
- **Page:** Ask → "Close October 2026" card; `/ledger`; `POST /api/ingest` (the Ask Upload card).
- **Steps:**
  1. Fresh seed at Nov 1. Import `ask-upload-sample.csv` from the Ask Upload card.
  2. Ask "Close October 2026", type a name, and click **Approve close of Oct 2026**. You get 201: "Closed Oct 2026 · pack 9f92… · chain #8".
  3. In Ask, upload a 2-row synthetic CSV with issue dates 2023-10-12 (CA, $733.10) and 2021-10-09 (TX, $615.25). The preview is clean, and the import reads "2 new stale items found".
  4. Ask "Close October 2026" again, then "Close November 2026".
- **Expected:**
  - A closed month's numbers never change.
  - Activity dated into a closed month posts as a prior-period adjustment in the open month (or the import is refused), and leaves a chain event.
  - The closed card shows the frozen pack's numbers.
- **Actual:**

  | | At close (frozen pack) | Same card after the backdated import |
  |---|---|---|
  | Card status | "Oct 2026 is closed · pack 9f92… · chain #8" | the same text |
  | Oct opening, total | $63,058.36 | **$64,406.71** |
  | Oct closing, total | $63,425.18 | **$64,773.53** |
  | CA / TX closing | $29,022.49 / $20,671.49 | $29,755.59 / $21,286.74 |
  | Tie-out line | "every state ties" | "every state ties" |

  - Nov's opening is now $64,773.53. That does not equal the frozen Oct closing of $63,425.18; the $1,348.35 gap is exactly the two backdated rows.
  - The rows were recognized in Apr 2022 and Apr 2024, which are new months in the Ledger dropdown. Nothing shows up as an adjustment.
  - The frozen ZIP is still byte-identical (50,446 bytes, sha256 `1e8f4666…` before and after). The pack is safe; the books and the card are not.
- **Why it matters:**
  - The demo pitch is "Closed Oct 2026 · in the chain at #8". An auditor who reopens the card sees different totals under the word "closed", and the tie-out still says everything ties.
  - The import that caused the change is not in the chain either (R11-07).
- **Screenshots:** `card-close-oct-done-1440.png`, `card-october-after-backdated-1440.png`, `card-november-after-backdated-1440.png`, `card-upload-result-375.png`
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Once a period is closed, post any recognition dated on or before its end into the first open month as a "prior-period adjustment (closed Oct 2026)" line.
  - For a closed period, render the card's tie-out from the frozen manifest. If the live roll-forward differs, add one line: "Live books differ by $1,348.35 since close (2 backdated items) · posted to Nov 2026".
- **Files:** `src/lib/ledger/rollforward.ts` (JE grouping), `src/lib/close-pack/close.ts`, `src/lib/ask/cards/approval-data.ts` (period close card data), `src/components/chat/views/ask/PeriodCloseView.tsx`

#### R11-02 · An approved letter can be approved again after a reload; the second name replaces the first
- **Page:** Ask → "Review approvals" card; `POST /api/outreach/[id]/approve`; `/approvals`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Ask "Draft outreach" → **Request approval** on #10087. The letter is composed.
  2. Ask "Review approvals". On #10087, type "Dogfood Controller" and click **Approve**. You get "Approved by a person"; chain row #4 is `approval.granted`.
  3. Reload `/ask`. The transcript comes back from session storage, and the #10087 row shows the name field and **Approve** again.
  4. Type "Second Person" and click Approve.
- **Expected:** The row restores as "Approved by Dogfood Controller". If it does not, the server refuses ("already approved", 409), as it does for a re-close (see What worked well).
- **Actual:**
  - `201 {"status":"approved", …}` with the same payload hash, and chain row #5 is a second `approval.granted` for the same letter.
  - On `/approvals?sel=…` the approver now reads **"Second Person (passcode session …)"**. Dogfood Controller's approval appears only in Activity and the chain.
  - Nothing was sent, and the content hash is unchanged, so no approval was bypassed. But the record of who approved the letter was silently replaced.
- **Screenshots:** `card-review-approved-row-1440.png`, `card-review-after-reload-1440.png`, `approvals-list-after-double-approve-1440.png`
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Server: refuse approve on an outreach row that already has an unconsumed approval for the same hash, with 409 `already_approved` and "Approved by X at …".
  - Client: restored cards re-read their row status on mount with a GET (no POST), or render restored approval controls inert with "Reload this card".
- **Files:** `src/app/api/outreach/[id]/approve/route.ts`, `src/lib/approvals.ts`, `src/components/chat/views/ask/ReviewApprovalsView.tsx`, `src/components/chat/views/ask/ApproveControl.tsx`

  2. Open any card ("get started", "set up integrations", …). Each card says "Answered directly from your data; no model was used" and "Setup card · no model".

## What worked well (keep in the demo)
- **Every card is the page's twin, and the server enforces everything.**
  - The Review card's Approve made exactly one POST to `/api/outreach/{id}/approve`. Chain row #4 is `approval.granted` with `mail_letter` and payload `8c64713b…`, and `/approvals?sel=…` shows the same approver, the same payload hash and a separate Send step.
  - API probes:
    - Approve: no body 400; empty or one-character name 400 ("Enter your name (2+ characters)"); wrong hash 409 `reviewed_content_changed`; missing hash 400.
    - Close: no name 400; wrong journal hash 409.
    - Remit: wrong amount 409.
    - Anonymous `/api/chat`: 401.
  - Refused calls added no chain rows.
- **Double-clicks are safe.** Approve, Request approval, Import and Approve close each produced one POST.
- **A force-enabled button is still safe.** Removing the `disabled` attribute and clicking with an empty name, or with the "approve anyway" box unticked, sent nothing.
- **Month close is clean on its own terms.**
  - Closing Oct wrote chain #6 `approval.granted` and #7 `approval.consumed` (`close_period`), then #8 `period.closed` with `journalSha256` and `manifestSha256`.
  - Re-asking showed "Oct 2026 is closed · pack 9f92… · chain #8 · by Dogfood Controller" with no Approve button.
  - A restored stale card's re-close got 409 "Oct 2026 is already closed … it cannot be re-closed", shown verbatim on the card.
  - The frozen pack re-downloads byte-identical. "Close November 2026" honestly says "not over on the demo clock: this close covers through Nov 1".
- **Upload is the best first-run moment.**
  - Mapping is suggested from non-standard headers (Vendor, Date Issued, Chk No).
  - The dry-run preview reads "4 ready / 2 rejected", and the row errors cite spreadsheet lines ("Line 5 amount: cannot parse "not-a-number"", "Line 6 … after today").
  - Nothing is stored until Import. The 4 rows really land: Items #90001–#90003 and #90006 appear, and the dashboard total moves from $61,915.00 to **$63,875.69**, which is $61,915.00 plus exactly the four amounts.
- **The approve-anyway flow from the card is right.**
  - On the TX report, Approve stays disabled until the "20 UNVERIFIED rule values … 2 required due-diligence notices were never sent" box is ticked.
  - The server stored `unverifiedAcknowledged: 20, unsentAcknowledged: 2`.
  - The File card then routes only to "Open the replica filing runner". The scripted filing agent finished in 6.3 s (REPL-TX-2026-K8NX4A).
- **Free text is honest.** With no key, the poem, "How much do we owe Texas?" and the dormancy question cannot be sent, and the hint explains why. Nothing was ever answered from memory.
- **The switch remembers and the shortcuts work.**
  - Dashboard → `/items?state=TX` → Ask → Dashboard returns to `/items?state=TX`.
  - `⌘/` and `Ctrl+/` toggle, including while typing in the composer. `.` runs the next action from Ask and from Dashboard. The `?` sheet lists "Switch between Ask and Dashboard ⌘/".
  - The 375 menu sheet has 19 links (every page plus sign-out) and closes on navigation.
- **Accessibility and the hard rules.**
  - All 8 cards are axe-clean with 0 overflow at 1440 and 375.
  - The sweep of 14 routes × 2 widths found 0 axe serious or critical issues, 0 overflow, 0 console errors, no green computed color, and the disclaimer on every screen, including Ask.
  - The 3D ornament loads lazily: poster plus an `aria-hidden` canvas at 1440, poster only under reduced motion, and nothing at 375.
- **The calm dashboard passes the 10-second test.** "$65,224.04 owed to its owners", "2 overdue" in coral, and one coral CTA. Secondary panels sit in a single "More detail" fold that is axe-clean when open.

## Biggest opportunities (to impress a demo judge)
1. **"Closed means closed" (R11-01, R11-04, R11-07).**
   - Lock closed periods. A backdated import posts a "Prior-period adjustment · closed Oct 2026 · +$1,348.35" line into the open month and writes an `ingestion.ledger` chain event.
   - The closed card renders the frozen numbers and says "live books differ by $1,348.35 since close → posted to Nov 2026". Months close in order.
   - The judge then sees a controller-grade close that an auditor can re-tie, not just a frozen ZIP.
2. **A keyless golden path in six coral chips (R11-05, R11-06).**
