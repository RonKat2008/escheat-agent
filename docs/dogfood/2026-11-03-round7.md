### P1

#### R7-01 · CA two-stage: after the notice filing, the remit stage can never be filed or remitted, so the item goes overdue with no way out
- **Page:** `/close-out/CA`, `/reports?state=CA` and the dashboard, after a demo-clock jump.
- **Steps:**
  1. Fresh seed. File CA through the runner on Nov 1, 2026 (Callum Whitlock #10327, $1,574.10, the CA **notice** report).
  2. The receipt correctly says "Next stage: Remit report due Jun 15, 2027. This notice report carries no money." The remit step is grey: "Not due yet… Remit report due Jun 15, 2027 (UNVERIFIED)".
  3. Use the demo clock to go to Jun 1, 2027, then Jun 20, 2027, then Mar 30, 2029. Open the runner and Reports each time.
- **Expected:** When the remit report comes due, the runner (and Reports) offers the CA remit stage for Callum: generate the remit report, approve, file, Record remittance. Round 6 opportunity #2 asked for exactly this.
- **Actual:**
  - **Jun 1, 2027.** The runner has moved on to the *next* notice cycle: Ravenhill and Yarrowdale, $3,484.10, "0 of 8". Its remit step reads "Remittance follows the submitted report. Due **Jun 15, 2028**". Callum does not appear anywhere in the runner. Reports shows "Stage: notice · To report 2 · $3,484.10". Items lists Callum as "Reported · next deadline Jun 15, 2027 · in 14d".
  - **Jun 20, 2027.** The dashboard turns pink: "OVERDUE · Callum Whitlock #10327, CA $1,574.10 · Remit due Jun 15, 2027, 5 days late · ~$2.59 interest accruing (CA)". Reports adds a banner: "CA remittance is overdue · Remit due Jun 15, 2027, 5 days late (1 item, $1,574.10)". But there is no Generate for a remit stage, and no **Record remittance** on Reports or in the runner.
  - **Mar 30, 2029.** The headline overdue list leads with "Callum Whitlock… Remit due Jun 15, 2027, **654 days late**". Interest is still accruing.
  - A controller who did everything right in November ends up with an ever-growing late remittance, and the app gives them no way to clear it.
- **Cause:** `remittanceOwed` returns `null` whenever the latest filed report's stage does not remit (`filedStageRemits(latest) === false`), so Record remittance never renders for a notice item. The runner builds its cycle from `to_report` items only, so a `reported` item waiting on its remit stage belongs to no cycle. The R6-02 fix stopped offering remittance too early, but nothing offers it later.
- **Screenshots:** `co7-CA-99-final.png` (the Nov 1 receipt with "Next stage"), `k1-close_out_CA-1440.png`, `k1-reports_state_CA-1440.png`, `k2-dashboard-1440.png`, `k2-reports_state_CA-1440.png`, `k2-items_state_CA_overdue_1-1440.png`, `n1-dashboard-1440.png`
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Treat "reported, remit stage due" as its own runner cycle for CA. Steps: "Remit report: generate → approve → file → Record remittance". Show it before the new notice cycle once `remitDue` is within the window or past it.
  - In `remittanceOwed`, allow a remittance when the *items'* remit stage is due, even though the last filed report was the notice.
  - At minimum, show Record remittance on Reports next to the "CA remittance is overdue" banner.
- **Files:** `src/lib/close-out/remittance.ts:90-112` (`remittanceOwed`), `src/lib/close-out/runner.ts` (`loadRunnerFacts`, the cycle's item set and the `stage`/`nextStageOf` wiring), `src/lib/close-out/runner-model.ts` (remit step), `src/app/(app)/reports/ReportsView.tsx:292-357`

### P3

#### R7-07 · Owner-wording guard: a condition anywhere in the sentence switches off the negation check, and keyword salad still passes
The guard now refuses round 6's sentence and two variants, with a clear message:
- "We are not holding any property… never deliver…"
- "…isn't holding property… You don't need to contact us…"
- "We hold no property… nothing will be delivered…"

Each gets "This sentence denies what the statute requires it to say (…). Rewrite it… without "not", "never" or "do not"".

These still pass all six ticks with **Approve** enabled:
- "We are holding a check for you. We will **not** deliver it to the Texas Comptroller by July 1 **even if** you never claim it."
  - Because the clause contains "if", `hasUnnegated` treats the whole clause as conditional and ignores the "not".
  - A library check confirms it: `hasUnnegated("We will not deliver it to the Texas Comptroller even if you never claim it", /Comptroller/i) === true`.
- "Hold property claim Texas Comptroller July 1 we" (keyword salad).
- "There is no reason to claim. We're holding property for you, and it goes to the Texas Comptroller on or before July 1 unless you contact us."

A person still approves, and the adoption itself worked: "Approved by Dogfood Controller. Saved as rule version… the checked-in rule file is unchanged", and it shows the honest unlock "No TX letter can be drafted yet. Next TX notice opens May 4, 2029: Tallowby Metalworks ($450.51)".

Fix:
- Only skip the negation check when the condition word comes *before* the negator in the clause ("If it is not claimed…"), not anywhere in the clause.
- Require at least one finite verb near "holding" (or a minimum word count) to reject salad.
- Consider a "Read it back" step that quotes the sentence.

## What worked well (keep in the demo)
- **The frozen receipt is the best audit artifact in the app.** It shows the confirmation (with a link to the replica), filed and remitted amounts, the report due date and days late, the interest with its rule version, the approver, the acknowledged unverified count, the never-sent notices and the validator warnings. The PDF matches the screen line for line, and recording the payment only adds to it.
- **The runner closes a state fast and keeps the human in charge.** TX took 35 s end to end (filing agent 15.6 s) and CA 39 s including letters. Highlights:
  - letters read inline before approval
  - pink "Not sent" steps for notices that never went out, which the approval must acknowledge
  - "Cycle closed · $773.74 remitted" at the end
- **Approve-anyway is server-enforced on Reports too.** A direct `POST /api/reports/:id/approve` without the acknowledgement returns 409 `{"code":"unverified_not_acknowledged","required":{"unverified":15,"unsent":1}}`. In the UI, Approve stays disabled until the box is ticked.
- **Chat as a closing beat.** After a 2.5-year clock jump and three filings, "What do we owe and where?" answers $56,760.83 across 44 items live, offline, labeled as no-model, and the figure matches the dashboard to the cent.
- **The Judge tour.** All 10 stops were ready in about 1 s at 1440 and 375. Readouts are page text. The judge's own recovery search turns $0 into $1,180.00 in about 9 s, and that is the only POST the tour sends.
- **The state's side of the pipe.** The replica TX holder dashboard lists the filed report, and the search portal lists the filed claim, both with the confirmation numbers the app printed.
- **The owner loop.** The owner page is in plain English ("We are holding $2,194.70 for you… Respond by (estimated) May 2, 2027"), axe-clean at 375 and 1440, and single-use ("This link was already used"). Approvals then shows "Reissue $2,194.70 to Ravenhill Cabinetry" with "OWNER REPLIED · NOV 1, 2026".
- **Resets.** Both take 1.6 s. The clock reset's toast now accounts for every kept action and for filings dated in the future. Reset demo data is guarded, confirmed and honest about what it wipes.
- **Mechanics.** 174 sweep renders with zero errors, overflow and axe issues, and the palette held inside the app shell.

## Biggest opportunities (to impress finance/ops judges)
1. **Finish California (R7-01).** Make "Remit report due Jun 15, 2027" a real runner cycle that a +1y jump unlocks: generate the remit report for the notice items, approve, file on the replica, Record remittance. Then a judge who knows California sees the two-stage cycle done right from November to June. Today the app flags the item as late and offers no way to act.
2. **One close-out story everywhere (R7-02, R7-03).** Feed the runner's step tones (`missed`, `not_needed`, late) into chat's card, re-record the `closeout` cassettes on a fresh seed, and add a `chat:verify` gate so a pristine demo never says "the data changed".
3. **Stamp demo dates at write time (R7-04).** Store the demo date on reports, approvals, activity and rule versions when they are written, and show it. Clamping at display time cannot fix dates that are earlier than the demo date. A filing "generated Nov 4, 2026" and "filed 29 days late" for a 2029 deadline is the one contradiction an auditor would notice.
4. **Make Reset demo data equal to the seed (R7-05).** Re-run the offline eval so the tour's last stop always has its number. Add the eval run to `demoInvariantProblems`.
5. **Status-aware Approvals copy (R7-08, R7-09).** A sent, replied or cancelled letter should read like one. Putting the approver name inside the Discard dialog would also remove the one clumsy gate in the app.

## What I could not check
- **Live model behavior.** Everything ran with `ESCHEAT_OFFLINE=1` and no Anthropic, Lob, AgentMail or Vapi key.
- **`next build && next start` timings.** Dev mode only.
- **Evidence PDF layout.** I checked only its text layer (`pdfPageTexts`); there is no PDF renderer on this machine.
- **The `/runs` paging with 200+ rows.** The SQL filters and paging were verified by reading the code. Reset wiped the post-flow runs before I could page them.
- **The dashboard "Paid out" vs "Resolved with owner" label.** No item was marked resolved this round.

I had killed and restarted that server with a different env on the same `NEXT_DIST_DIR`. Deleting `.next-dogfood7` fixed it. I treat this as a dev-cache artifact, not a product bug, and none of the results above come from that server.
