#### R5-01 · The TX unlock's "2 TX letters can now be drafted" are the two items Texas does not require a notice for
- **Page:** Rules → TX → Required notice language → **Review statute and owner wording**; then Approvals, Items and `/close-out/TX`.
- **Steps:** Fresh seed. Open `/rules?state=TX&field=dueDiligence.requiredLanguage`, write an owner sentence, type a name, approve. Follow "Go to Approvals to compose and review the letters". Then Items → State TX → select all → **Draft outreach for 15 items** → **Draft 2 letters**, and compose them.
- **Expected:** The unlock count describes letters the law requires now, and following the CTA lands on them.
- **Actual:**
  - The dialog says "2 TX letters ($348.29) become draftable", and after approval "2 TX letters can now be drafted ($348.29)".
  - Those two are **Moonrake Industrial #10310 ($163.22)** and **Wrenfield Cabinetry #10519 ($185.07)**. Both are below `dueDiligence.minAmountCents = 25001` ("no owner notice is needed for $250 or less", § 74.1011), and the letter preview's own safety check says "The amount is below TX's due-diligence minimum ($250.01), so a letter may not be required."
  - They pass the bulk plan only because a sub-threshold item has no window, so the "window not open yet" skip never fires. Their next deadlines are Jul 3, 2028 and Jul 2, 2029.
  - Elsewhere the app says nothing was unlocked:
    - The CTA lands on an Approvals page with no TX letters.
    - Items shows TX "Draft-ready 0".
    - `/close-out/TX` says "No TX item is inside its due-diligence window right now".
  - When the two letters are sent, the owner page reads "Respond by **Unknown**".
  - This is the "rules feed letters" moment the tour (stop 4) sends judges to.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/shots/dogfood5/r-04-adopted.png`, `r-07-bulk-tx-preview.png`, `r-08-tx-letter-3af031.png`, `ap-05-owner-375.png`, `a-close-out_TX-1440.png`
- **Suggested fix:**
  - In the bulk plan, treat "below the state's due-diligence minimum" as its own skip ("No notice required under $250.01 (TX); optional courtesy letter"), and exclude those items from `computeImpact`.
  - Phrase the unlock as "Unblocks the notice wording for all 14 TX letters; next one opens Feb 23, 2027 (Rushmere Freight)", which is true and still a strong moment.
  - Point the CTA at `/approvals?state=TX` only when something is actually draftable.
- **Files:** `src/lib/rules/adopt.ts` (`computeImpact`), `src/lib/outreach/bulk-plan.ts` / `bulk.ts`, `src/app/(app)/rules/adoption-review.tsx`, `src/lib/close-out/plan.ts`

#### R5-03 · The close-out runner approves a letter the person never sees, and mislabels which letter it is
- **Page:** `/close-out/CA`, steps 2 and 3.
- **Steps:**
  1. On a fresh seed, run the chat demo (which drafts and composes Ravenhill #10087).
  2. Open `/close-out/CA` and click **Compose 1 letter**. This composes Yarrowdale #10112, which is blocked with no mailing address.
  3. Type a name, then **Approve 1 letter**, then **Send 1 letter (Lob test mode)**.
- **Expected:** A person reads each letter before approving it (the tour's stop 5 says "You read each letter exactly as it will go out"). The step names the letter and amount it acts on.
- **Actual:**
  - After composing, the banner says "Composed 1 letter: **0 ready for your approval**, 1 need fixing". The step below it says "**1 letter is composed and ready for your approval** … 1 other draft need fixing", with the blocked letter's amount, **$1,289.40**.
  - Approving approved **Ravenhill ($2,194.70)**, a letter that was composed in chat 20 minutes earlier and never shown on this page. The runner has no preview, payee name or content hash.
  - The send step then mailed it and reported "**Sent 1 letter through Lob TEST mode. Nothing is mailed.**" The server ran with `ESCHEAT_OFFLINE=1`, and Approvals records the row as "Sent (offline — nothing mailed)". `runner-ops.ts` hard-codes the Lob wording and ignores the per-letter outcome.
  - The step then falls back to "Send the letters · Blocked · $1,289.40", and the progress stays at 2 of 8, so the screen shows no trace of the letter that went out.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/shots/dogfood5/co-CA-01-letters_approve.png`, `co-CA-03-letters_approve.png`, `co-CA-04-letters_send.png`, `ap-02-fcecaf5f.png`
- **Suggested fix:**
  - In the runner's approve and send steps, list each letter: payee, check #, amount, a "Preview" link to `/api/outreach/:id/preview` and the content hash. Require each letter to be opened, or link to `/approvals?kind=letter&state=CA` for the actual approval.
  - Use the send response's `outcome` ("offline" → "1 letter recorded as sent; offline mode, nothing was mailed").
  - Count a sent letter as a done sub-step.
  - Key the step's amount to the letters it acts on.
- **Files:** `src/app/(app)/close-out/[state]/CloseOutRunner.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/close-out/[state]/runner-ops.ts:14-24`, `src/lib/close-out/runner-model.ts:160-205`
