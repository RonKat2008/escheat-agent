#### R10-01 · A close pack's journal entry numbers change meaning after a clock move, so two packs for the same month disagree
- **Page:** `/ledger` → **Close pack** (Nov 2026), and `GET /api/close-pack?period=2026-11`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Fresh seed at Nov 1. Download the Nov 2026 close pack.
  2. File and remit TX, then advance the clock +30 and +15 days.
  3. Download the Nov 2026 pack again.
- **Expected:** A JE number that went out in a pack keeps its date, amount and lines. Later activity gets new JE numbers, or a prior-period adjustment if it is backdated into a month that was already packed.
- **Actual:**

  | | Pack at Nov 1 | Pack at Dec 16 |
  |---|---|---|
  | `JE-TX-202611-01` | **Nov 1**, $450.51, "1 item" | **Nov 22**, **$680.29**, "2 items" |
  | `JE-TX-202611-02` | absent | Nov 1, $773.74 remittance (numbered after an entry dated three weeks later) |
  | FL / IL / OR JEs | absent | Nov 2, Nov 3, Nov 7 (items detected on Dec 1, posted back into November) |
  | Roll-forward closing | $61,915.00 (period Nov 1–Nov 1) | $64,580.20 (period Nov 1–Nov 30) |

  - Both packs say "All checks tie", and both are internally consistent. Side by side, they disagree on what `JE-TX-202611-01` is.
  - The JE is still dated at its group's latest item (Nov 22), which is the behavior R8-02 was meant to remove, and its id moves with it.
  - The first pack is labelled "Nov 2026" even though it covers one day; nothing says "month to date" (see R10-06).
- **Why it matters:** the pack is pitched as "what a controller hands an auditor". An auditor who ties the second pack to the first finds a different entry under the same number. The chain proves the event log was not edited, but the journal it summarizes was rewritten.
- **Screenshots:** `cp10-n0-ledger-1440.png`, `cp10-n2-ledger-1440.png`, `n10-2-ledger-month-1440.png`
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Give each JE a stable id when it is first posted, one per detection batch, dated on the detection day, and never regroup it.
  - Add "Close Nov 2026": freeze the period's JEs and append the pack's manifest hash to the audit chain. Later detections that are backdated into a closed month then post as prior-period adjustments in the open month.
  - Label packs for the current month "month to date".
- **Files:** `src/lib/ledger/rollforward.ts` (or the JE grouping in `src/lib/ledger/`), `src/lib/close-pack/build.ts`, `src/lib/close-pack/period.ts`

#### R10-03 · The chain blocks UPDATE but not DELETE, and deleting the newest rows leaves "Chain verified"
- **Page:** `/activity` → "Chain verified · N events" popover; the `audit_chain` table.
- **What I checked (without touching the live DB):**
  - On a copy of the database: `audit_chain_no_update` (BEFORE UPDATE) is the only trigger. Both UPDATEs I tried were refused ("audit_chain is append-only: rows cannot be updated").
  - No trigger covers DELETE. The table also cascades on company delete, and a seed or demo reset wipes it, which is expected for the demo.
- **Why it matters:**
  - `verifyRows` checks each `seq`/`prevHash` link from the genesis hash. Any prefix of a valid chain is itself a valid chain. So deleting the last k rows (for example a `remittance.recorded` or an `approval.granted`) leaves a badge reading "Chain verified · N−k events".
  - The popover says "An edited, deleted or reordered row breaks it". That holds for every row except the newest ones.
  - The head anchored in an earlier binder, Activity CSV or close pack catches this only if someone compares the files by hand. The app never does.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Add a BEFORE DELETE trigger, with the reset path dropping and recreating it explicitly.
  - Narrow the popover copy ("deleting the newest rows is caught only by comparing with an exported head").
  - Offer "Check a file": drop in a binder, CSV or pack, and the app confirms its head hash is still in the chain at the stated length.
- **Files:** `drizzle/0014_audit_chain.sql`, `src/lib/audit/chain.ts`, `src/app/(app)/activity/ChainBadge.tsx`
