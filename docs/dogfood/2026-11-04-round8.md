### P1

#### R8-01 · After a clock jump, newly stale money shows on Horizon only; Dashboard, Ledger and chat understate the liability
- **Page:** `/horizon`, `/dashboard`, `/ledger`, `/chat`, `/activity` after `+90 days`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Fresh seed. File and remit TX through the runner (n1: $61,141.26 across 46 items everywhere).
  2. Advance the demo clock 90 days to Jan 30, 2027.
  3. Open Horizon, Dashboard, Ledger (FY2027), Items and chat ("What do we owe and where?").
- **Expected:** Payments that crossed the dormancy line are detected (or the app offers a pink "Run detection" action), and every page reports the same liability. At minimum, every page says the same thing about the 8 items.
- **Actual:**
  - The clock response lists `transitions: []`.
  - Horizon says "8 more ($4,386.00) already crossed the stale line and wait for the next detection run."
  - Dashboard, Items and chat still say $61,141.26 across 46. Ledger FY2027 shows Identified $0.00.
  - No page offers a way to run detection, so the "next detection run" never comes. The finance lead's number is $4,386.00 short and only one page knows.
- **Cause:** advancing the clock re-evaluates existing items but does not ingest or classify the outstanding payments that became stale. Horizon projects those payments itself, so it is the only page that sees them.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/shots/dogfood8/n8-2-horizon-1440.png`, `.data/shots/dogfood8/n8-2-dashboard-1440.png`, `.data/shots/dogfood8/n8-2-ledger-1440.png`, `.data/shots/dogfood8/n8-2-chat-owe-1440.png`
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Run detection as part of `advance` (offline, deterministic) so the 8 items land in the ledger as "Identified" on their stale date. Report them in the clock toast ("8 items went stale, $4,386.00").
  - Or add a pink "Run detection now" button on Horizon and Dashboard, and show "8 waiting for detection" next to the Dashboard headline until it runs.
  - Add "Horizon's crossed total equals 0 after advance" to the demo invariants.
- **Files:** `src/lib/clock.ts`, `src/app/api/clock/route.ts`, `src/lib/pipeline/` (detection), `src/app/(app)/horizon/HorizonView.tsx`, `src/lib/horizon/project.ts`

#### R8-02 · Ledger summary entries are dated at the group's latest item, so period exports disagree
- **Page:** `/ledger?period=fy` vs `/ledger?period=month`, and `/api/ledger.csv`.
- **Steps:** Fresh seed. Download the FY JE CSV and the month CSV, and compare them with the month roll-forward.
- **Expected:** Each JE falls in the period its items were identified. A controller can import month exports one after another without duplicates or gaps.
- **Actual:**
  - FY JE-006, "Void stale vendor checks (TX): 4 items, $3,348.87", is dated **Nov 1, 2026**. The month roll-forward says only **+$450.51** was identified in November.
  - FY JE-004 (CA, 7 items) is dated Sep 24 but covers items from February to September.
  - JE numbers restart at `JE-1` in every export, so two exports collide on import.
  - The roll-forward itself ties; only the journal is misdated.
- **Cause:** `buildJournal` groups movements by `kind|state|propertyType` across the whole period and dates each JE with `g.reduce((d, m) => (m.date > d ? m.date : d), first.date)`.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/shots/dogfood8/np0-ledger-fy-1440.png`, `.data/shots/dogfood8/t8-1440-11-ledger.png`
- **Suggested fix:** group by month too (`kind|state|propertyType|yyyy-mm`) and date each JE at the month end, or emit one JE per movement. Derive JE ids from content (for example `JE-2026-11-TX-ID-01`) so they are stable across exports.
- **Files:** `src/lib/ledger/journal.ts`, `src/lib/ledger/csv.ts`, `src/app/api/ledger.csv/route.ts`
