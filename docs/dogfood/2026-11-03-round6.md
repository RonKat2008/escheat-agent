#### R6-01 · Recording the remittance erases the late filing and the "approve anyway" from the receipt and the evidence pack
- **Page:** `/close-out/TX` (and `/close-out/DE`), the receipt, then **Evidence pack (PDF)**.
- **Steps:** Fresh seed. On `/close-out/TX`: Generate and validate, type a name, tick the acknowledgement, **Approve anyway**, **Run filing agent**. Read the receipt. Then **Record remittance** (reference `WIRE-DF6-TX`) and read the receipt and the PDF again.
- **Expected:** The filing record is fixed once the report is filed. The receipt and the pack keep "Report due Jul 1, 2026, filed 123 days late", the interest disclosed, and "14 still UNVERIFIED; approved anyway", and add the remittance line.
- **Actual:**
  - Before the remittance, the receipt is right: "Report due Jul 1, 2026, **filed 123 days late** · Late interest disclosed **~$26.07** accruing … UNVERIFIED · Rule values relied on **14 still UNVERIFIED; approved anyway**".
  - After **Record remittance**, the same receipt reads "Report due **UNKNOWN** · Late interest disclosed **None: nothing in this filing is late** · Rule values relied on **All verified**".
  - The evidence pack downloaded afterwards says the same: "Report due: UNKNOWN", "Late interest disclosed: None: nothing in this filing is late.", "To remit: due date UNKNOWN".
  - DE at Mar 30, 2029 repeats it. The runner showed "Late · due Mar 1, 2029, 29 days ago" and "15 UNVERIFIED", but the receipt and pack after the remittance say "UNKNOWN", "nothing in this filing is late" and "All verified".
  - The same thing makes the "Verify the rules" step turn blue "Done · Every rule value this cycle relies on is verified" (see R6-05).
  - The auditor's document understates a late filing and contradicts the controller's own acknowledgement. This is the round-5 opportunity #2 working in reverse.
- **Cause:** `loadRunnerFacts` derives the report-due date, days late, interest and unverified count from the state's *current* `to_report`/`reported` items and overdue list. Once the items are `remitted`, the sets are empty, so every field falls back to UNKNOWN/none/0.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/shots/dogfood6/co6-TX-4-remit-before.png` (before), `co6-TX-4-record_remittance.png`, `co6-TX-99-final.png` (after), `co6-DE-99-final.png`
- **Suggested fix:** Snapshot the filing facts on the report row at submit time (report due, days late, interest figure and its rule version, the acknowledged unverified count, the approver), and have the receipt and the evidence pack read the snapshot, never the live item set.
- **Files:** `src/lib/close-out/runner.ts:118-147` (`loadRunnerFacts`), `src/lib/close-out/runner-model.ts:458-480` (`receiptOf`), `src/lib/reports/evidence-pack.ts:280-300`

#### R6-06 · Agent runs only reads the newest 200 rows, so chat, filing and recovery runs vanish behind classifier runs
- **Page:** `/runs`, `/runs?agent=chat`.
- **Steps:** After the flows (16 succeeded runs, including 8 chat runs, 3 filing runs and a recovery run), add 210 newer classifier runs and reload. I inserted them directly into this scratch DB to simulate a 210-item ledger import. Each classified item writes one `classifier` run through `callStructured`, so a real CSV import with that many stale lines does the same.
- **Actual:**
  - `/runs?agent=chat` reads "No runs match these filters. Clear filters".
  - The **Chat**, Filing agent, Claim agent, Recovery agent and Letter writer chips disappear from the agent filter. Only "Classifier" is left.
  - The status counts read "Succeeded 200", although the database holds 226.
  - There is no "load more": `showAll` only shows more rows from the same 200.
- **Cause:** `RunsList` calls `listAgentRuns(db, companyId, { limit: 200 })` and then applies the agent, status and replay filters in memory. The chat tool's `listAgentRunsTool` passes `agent` but has the same post-limit filter.
- **About `tests/e2e/ui-activity-r2.spec.ts` "replayed chat runs are labeled and not billed":** the spec itself is not at risk today. The ui project runs with `workers: 1`, `startedAt` is `defaultNow()` (the real clock, not the demo clock), and the spec loads `/runs` immediately after its chat turn, so its chat run is the newest row. It would only go missing if 200 or more runs were written between the chat turn and the page load. The UX bug is real, though: any history older than the newest 200 rows of *all* agents cannot be reached, and the agent filter can claim there are none. Fix it with a `where agent = ?` (and status) in the query and paginate with an offset, rather than by changing the spec.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/shots/dogfood6/ru6-d-_runs_agent_chat.png`, `ru6-d-_runs.png`, compared with `ru6-b-_runs_agent_chat.png`
- **Files:** `src/lib/queries/runs.ts:5-14` (`listAgentRuns`), `src/app/(app)/runs/RunsList.tsx:95-104`, `src/lib/chat/tools/actions.ts:176`

## What worked well (keep in the demo)
- **The close-out runner is the best screen in the app.** TX went from Generate to a replica receipt and a recorded remittance in **18 s**, with:
  - an approve-anyway step that names the exact checksum and the unverified count
  - a live filmstrip of the filing agent signing in, uploading, pausing for the human approval and submitting
  - a Late chip on every overdue step

  CA added letters read inline in the runner before approval. DE closed in 18 s at Mar 2029.
- **The TX rule review.** It shows "Statute says (verbatim)" with three sources next to an empty "Owner letter will say", and a six-tick checklist that refuses bad wording live. The approval reports what it actually unlocks and does not touch git. This is the clearest "the model never decides the law" moment.
- **The Judge tour.** All 10 stops were ready in about 1.5 s each at 1440 and 375, axe-clean on the stops checked. The judge's own **Run recovery search** click at stop 7 turns "$0.00" into "$1,180.00" in 8 s. Nothing else is posted.
- **One set of numbers.** The headline, Items, the Money Flow, the Money Map and the fresh-seed chat answer agreed to the cent at every checkpoint.
- **The owner loop.** The owner page is in plain English with "Respond by (estimated)" and a single-use link. "Send me the money" becomes "Reissue $2,194.70 to Ravenhill Cabinetry" in Approvals, then Mark resolved, and the item leaves the headline.
- **The recovery claim:** a typed approval, then **File claim**, then `REPL-CLM-TX-2026-GAYHET` in 7.6 s, and the Recovery flow moves to "Waiting on the state".
- **Mechanics:** 162 clean renders, a 0.7 s Reset, and honest offline wording everywhere ("recorded as sent in offline mode; nothing was mailed").

## Biggest opportunities (to impress finance/ops judges)
1. **Make the filing record immutable and complete (R6-01, R6-07, R6-09, R6-13).**
   - Snapshot the filing facts at submit time: due date, days late, interest and its rule version, the acknowledged unverified count, the approver, any due-diligence notice that was never sent, and the warnings.
   - Have the receipt, the Reports detail and the evidence pack all print that snapshot plus the remittance.
   - A controller can then hand the PDF to an auditor and have it match the screen line for line. This is the single most "real" thing in the demo, and right now recording the payment rewrites it.
2. **Model CA's two-stage cycle in the runner (R6-02).** After the notice filing, show "Notice filed Nov 1, 2026 · remit report due Jun 15, 2027 (UNVERIFIED)" as a scheduled step. Let a +1y clock jump turn it into "Remit report: generate, approve, file, record remittance". A finance judge who knows California will notice the difference.
3. **Keep chat alive through the demo (R6-03, R6-04).** Answer the deterministic questions (exposure, due in 90 days, explain #, close-out plan) live from the database in offline mode instead of via cassettes, and keep cassettes only for model prose. Then chat can be the closing beat of the tour ("What do we still owe?" → $54,566.13 after the filings), not a dead end.
4. **Show the filing from the state's side.** Add a "Filed reports" list to the replica holder dashboard (confirmation, date, items, total, checksum) and link it from the receipt ("View on the replica TX portal"). Do the same for claims. Judges then see both ends of the pipe.
5. **Agent runs as an audit log (R6-06, R6-11, R6-19).** Filter in SQL, paginate, and keep every agent chip. Open on a meaningful first row, such as the scripted filing run. Label the seed's heuristic classifications for what they are. A judge who clicks "Every agent run, logged" should never see "No runs match these filters".
